Basic Rules

1. The game is played by two teams, each with 11 players.
2. The goal is to score by kicking the ball into the other team’s net.
3. A match lasts 90 minutes, split into two halves of 45 minutes.
4. Players cannot use their hands, except the goalkeeper inside their box.
5. Fouls like pushing, tripping, or handball give the other team a free kick or penalty.
6. If the ball crosses the sidelines, play restarts with a throw‑in by the team that didn’t touch it last.
7. If the ball crosses the goal line but not into the net, play restarts with either a goal kick (if last touched by the attacking team) or a corner kick (if last touched by the defending team).
8. The offside rule prevents attackers from staying too close to the opponent’s goal without the ball — they must be level with or behind the second‑last defender when receiving a pass.
9. Yellow cards are given as warnings for unsporting behavior; two yellows equal a red card, which means the player is sent off.
10. A red card can also be given directly for serious fouls, violent conduct, or denying a clear goal‑scoring opportunity with a handball or foul.
