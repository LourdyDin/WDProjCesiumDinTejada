Early Origins of Football

Football traces back to ancient ball games played in the early civilizations such as China. It was called cuju, and it had a round ball stiched leather with fur or feathers inside and it was the first known ball game that involves kicking.

How the Rules were Made

In December 1863 the Football Association was founded in London to bring order to the game. The first official Laws of the game were written which banned rough practices like hacking and setting clear rules for play. This is where it marked the beginning of modern football

How Football Took Over the World

Football was no longer a random game but a national one. It was spreading fast with clubs popping up across Europe and South America. The game became a global obsession that people played and loved even wherever they are.

Football in the Modern era

Today as of December 16, 2025, football is more tha a sport but a worldwide celebration. Superstars like Pele, Maradona, Messi, Ronaldo have inspired millions with their talent. Football unites fans everywhere with passion and excitement
