Purpose of Website

Football Made Easy was created to make the history and rules of the game simple and enjoyable to learn. We break down complex stories into clear explanations so that anyone—whether a beginner or a fan—can appreciate football’s journey. Our goal is to share knowledge in a way that feels engaging, fun, and easy to follow.

Who We Are

This website was built by two creators, Lourdy and Sam, from the Philippine Science High School Main Campus (Pisay). Sam is part of the school’s football team, and after classes, both Lourdy and Sam often play together on the field. These experiences inspired us to share our passion for football with you, combining personal stories with knowledge to make learning about the sport more relatable.

How to Get Involved

You can join our community by exploring and sharing our content. We welcome feedback, ideas, and even stories from fellow fans. Together, we can build a friendly space that celebrates football knowledge and connects people who love the game.

Our Mission

Our mission is to inspire curiosity and a lifelong love for football. We aim to connect people worldwide by showing how the sport has evolved and why it matters. By keeping our explanations simple and engaging, we hope to spark interest and create fans who will carry their passion for football into the future.
