Purpose of Website

Football Made Easy is a informative website that gives the fundamentals of football(Soccer). With short and clear explanations, it breaks down the complex aspects of the game such as history, player positions, rules, and Teams . The website offers a clean layout and interactive features that makes the learning both easy and enjoyable. Football Made Easy is a reliable and engaging starting point on your journey into the world of football.

What is Football?

Football is a game that involves two teams of 11 players that are on the field who try to maneuver the ball into the other team’s goal without using their hands or arms(besides the goalkeeper). The team with the most goals wins. The sport can be played almost anywhere, from football fields to gymnasiums and even to the beach.
